1Name your Deliverable/Responsibility

Enter the deliverable or responsibility area you're mapping below. Pick one — don't try to boil the ocean.

2List the Steps

Click "+ Add Step" to add a box for each major step from start to finish. Add phases if it helps organize a longer process.

3Tag & Color-Code

Click the color dot on a step to mark it Green if AI/automation already exists there, or Gray if not yet.

4Spot AI Opportunities

Revisit every Gray step — could AI support or automate it? Capture ideas in Notes. Drag from a step's side handle to another step to connect them.
